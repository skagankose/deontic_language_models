# Synthetic benchmark: dataset statistics

Source file: `bench/data/scenarios.jsonl` (generated by `bench/generate.py`, seed 42, 2026-11-25).

## Overview

- **4 queries**, one per legal domain.
- **240 evidence packages** in total, 60 per query.
- **120 distinct cases** (theories), 30 per query. Each case is written in **2 paraphrases** with different clause order, wording, and ways of expressing priorities. Both paraphrases always share the same gold answer.
- An **evidence package** is one complete set of source units: defined terms, contract clauses, schedule entries and case-file facts.
- Gold labels are computed by the `pcnr` solver from the generated gold theory. The data are fully synthetic: no real contracts were used and no lawyer reviewed them.
